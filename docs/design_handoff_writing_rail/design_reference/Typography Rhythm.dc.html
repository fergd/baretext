It Begins

They spray the insecticides. Then pesticides. They don't want them. Aphids on the tomatoes? Plant basil. Or marigolds. Keeps aphids away and it looks good, too. Same with carrots and onions. Onions keep the carrot flies off. Carrots keep the onion flies off. The people who lived here first knew that.

The waiting room had a rhythm to it. The monitor came in on the one, the vent on the two, and somewhere down the hall a cart with a bad wheel kept time badly. Focus, she thought. Focus.

"Could I sit for a minute?" Was that a yes? His head moved — ish. I sat.

"Look," I said, and heard my own voice go thin on the first word, "I rehearsed this. I know it doesn't mean much, and I know it doesn't make a difference." That wasn't part of what I'd rehearsed.

Meeting the Father

He did not look up. A door opened somewhere behind me and two people laughed at something small, and it was the worst sound I had ever heard.

"I'm sorry," I said. "I know I shouldn't have come." I'm too tired to be angry. Too tired.
