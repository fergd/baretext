Behavior: the switcher is a role="tablist" of two real buttons — keyboard: ←/→ to move, Enter to activate; ⌘K still switches modes too. Selecting a tab sets data-mode on the app root (same attr the app already reads) and swaps the mode's feature set. The active tab is a quiet raised chip (a subtle background lift, no accent fill) — legible but calm, so nothing in the bar competes with the writing.
