Typewriter mode

Toggled from ⌘K (⌘⇧T). The line you're writing locks to the vertical center and the page scrolls up beneath it — your eye never travels. 4a pairs it with focus dimming: everything but the active sentence fades back, so only the words in front of you stay lit. A faint center guide and the typewriter tag in the status bar are the only added chrome.

Editor mode

Same writing surface, more tools layered on — never disturbing the page underneath. 3a is the base: a dismissible find/replace bar and inline spellcheck. Scene cards are the open question, so here are two answers — 3b a persistent left rail for always-on navigation (and where the AI panel can live later), or 3c a full-screen corkboard you summon to restructure, then dismiss. Both nest scenes under their chapter — the rail as a collapsible tree, the board as chapter sections. Which fits how you work?
